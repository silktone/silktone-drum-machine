Silktone Drum Machine
User Manual

Audio Unit for Logic Pro and VST3 — version 1.0

The Silktone Drum Machine is an instrument plugin. In Logic Pro, load it in an instrument slot on a software instrument track, just like any synth. It makes its own sound — it does not need an audio input to play.

When it opens you see the transport bar across the top and seven tabs underneath: Sequencer, Pads, Mixer, Sampler, Library, Stems, Export.

Press Play to start. The pattern that loads by default is a house groove at 120 BPM. Press Stop to go back to the start. Press Play again while playing to pause where you are; press it once more to pick up from there.

1. Getting started

Install: copy Silktone Drum Machine.component into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ (Logic) or Silktone Drum Machine.vst3 into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/. Restart Logic, or rescan in Plug-In Manager. The Standalone app runs on its own with no DAW.

Following Logic: by default the plugin follows Logic's tempo and transport. Start Logic's transport and the drum machine starts with it, at Logic's tempo. To run it at its own tempo, turn off Sync to Host Tempo and Follow Host Transport (both are automatable parameters — see section 10).

3. Sequencer tab

Thirty rows, one per voice, left to right in steps. A lit step plays; a dark step is silent.

  • Click a step to turn it on. Click it again and it becomes an accent (orange, hits harder). Click a third time to clear it.
  • Row name — click it to hear that voice on its own.
  • M / S — mute or solo the row.
  • Left / right arrows — scroll the sound for that row through its whole family. A kick row walks through every kick in every kit; a snare row walks through every snare. The sound name in the Mixer tab updates to match.
  • CLR — clear that row.
  • Copy row / Paste row — pick the row in the dropdown, copy it, pick another row, paste it. Clear all empties the whole grid.

The orange highlight shows the step that is playing right now.

4. Pads tab

Thirty pads, one per voice. Tap a pad to play it. Tap near the top of the pad for a hard hit, near the bottom for a soft one.

Right-click a pad for a menu: favorite it, mute it, solo it, or clear the sample loaded on it. A star in the corner marks a favorite.

5. Mixer tab

One Logic-style strip per voice, left to right:

ControlWhat it does
FaderThe voice's volume
PANLeft / right
TONEA tilt EQ. Turn it right for brighter, left for darker. (In the web app this knob did nothing — here it works.)
SATSaturation. A little adds warmth and grit; a lot distorts
REV / DLYHow much of this voice goes to the hall reverb and to the tempo-synced echo. These are sends, Logic-style: the dry sound stays, and the effect is added on top
M / SMute / solo the channel
AUX M / SMute the sends, or solo the sends (soloing the sends drops the dry signal, so you hear the effect only — live only)
MeterThe thin bar at the right of each strip shows the level

Above the strips are the two returns: Reverb return (how loud the hall is overall) and Echo return (how loud the echo is overall).

6. Sampler tab

Put your own sounds on the pads.

Load a file: pick a voice, press Load audio file…, choose any WAV, AIFF, MP3, FLAC or OGG. The sample replaces the synth sound for that voice. Press Clear sample to go back to the synth.

Record: the plugin records from its sidechain input. In Logic, open the sidechain menu in the plugin header and choose your input — for example your Apollo / UAD input 1. (In the Standalone app, pick your interface in the audio settings.) Pick the voice, press Record, play or sing into the input — the INPUT meter shows the level — and press Record again to stop. The take lands on the voice as a sample.

Tempo lock: when on, the sample plays in time with the tempo — a loop recorded at one tempo follows the plugin when the tempo changes.

Chop: splits a sample into slices you can send to pads.

  1. Pick the voice with the sample.
  2. Choose Transient (cut where the sound attacks) or Meter / grid (cut on the bar, half bar, beat, eighth or sixteenth).
  3. Pick a sensitivity — Gentle, Balanced or Fine.
  4. Press Chop sample. The slices appear in the list.
  5. Pick a slice, pick a target voice, press Send selected slice to pad.

The slice keeps the tempo-lock setting of the sample it came from.

7. Library tab

Your own drum sounds, searchable.

  1. Press Scan a folder… and choose a folder of drum sounds on your Mac. The plugin walks the folder (including subfolders) and sorts every file into a voice by its name — kicks, snares, hats, toms, congas, tombak, darbuka, and so on.
  2. Type in the search box to narrow the list, or tick Favorites only.
  3. Pick a voice, select a sound, press Load selected onto voice — or double-click a sound to load it straight onto the chosen voice.
  4. Favorite stars the selected sound so you can find it again.

8. Stems tab

Drop in a full song or a drum loop and split it into six parts:

StemWhat it holds
LowKick and low end (below ~150 Hz)
Snare & ClapThe backbeat body (~180 Hz to 1 kHz)
PercussionShakers, congas, mid instruments (~1–6.5 kHz)
Hats & CymbalsTop-end air (above ~6.5 kHz)
CentreWhatever sits in the middle of the stereo image — usually the vocal
SidesThe stereo sides only — wide parts, doubles, reverb tails
This is a frequency-band filter split, not AI separation. It is useful for grabbing a kick, a hat, or a vocal-ish centre out of a loop — it will not cleanly isolate a single instrument the way a dedicated AI stem tool does.

For each stem you can Play it, Export WAV, or send it straight to a pad (pick the voice, press the arrow button).

9. Export tab

  • Export MIDI (.mid) — the pattern as MIDI notes on channel 10, 96 ticks per quarter note. Drop it into Logic and it will play any drum instrument.
  • Export loop WAV (4 bars) — the pattern rendered as a stereo WAV, 4 bars plus a short tail. No click, no sends on the render — the dry pattern.
  • Export per-voice stems (.zip) — one dry WAV per sounding voice, zipped.

10. Automation and saving

These can be automated from Logic, and they are saved with the project:

  • Tempo, Swing, Swing Interval, Time Signature, Kit, Master Volume, Click, Reverb Return, Echo Return, Sync to Host, Follow Host Transport, MIDI Out
  • Per voice: Volume, Tune, Pan, Tone, Saturation, Send Reverb, Send Echo

The pattern itself — steps, accents, mute/solo, favorites, and any samples you loaded — is saved with the Logic project (or the plugin's own preset), not as automation. Samples are stored inside the saved state, so a project reopens with its sounds intact.

MIDI Out: when on, the plugin also sends the pattern out as MIDI notes on channel 10, so Logic can record the groove or drive another drum instrument with it.

11. Troubleshooting

ProblemTry this
No soundCheck the track is record-enabled or monitored, the Master volume is up, and no channel is soloed by accident. The click is live-only.
Logic doesn't list the pluginCheck the bundle is in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/, then open Logic's Plug-In Manager and rescan. In Terminal, auval -v aumu SLM8 Sltn shows the validation result.
Recording hears nothingThe sidechain must be set in the plugin header (Logic) or the audio device in the settings (Standalone), and the input must be live. Watch the INPUT meter in the Sampler tab.
A sample plays at the wrong speedTurn Tempo lock on and make sure the tempo matches the tempo the loop was recorded at; or turn it off to play the sample at its natural speed.
The stem split sounds roughThat is expected — it is a filter split, not AI. The Low stem is the cleanest; Centre and Sides are the roughest.

12. What the plugin is (and isn't)

  • It is the Silktone drum machine as a native instrument: same 30 voices, 31 kits, 166 patterns, same sequencer, mixer, sampler, library and exports.
  • The reverb is an algorithmic hall, not the web app's impulse convolver — close in character, not bit-identical.
  • The TONE knob works here; in the web app it did nothing.
  • Favorites are saved with the session here; in the web app they lasted only until the page was closed.
  • There is no gain knob — Joel asked for one, then dropped it.
